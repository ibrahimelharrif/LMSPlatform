About OSACMA

Learning the CMA with understanding

OSACMA is an online learning platform dedicated to the Certified Management Accountant (CMA). We turn every exam topic into short video lessons, downloadable materials, exam-style quizzes and a community that keeps you moving until exam day.

🎯

1. Learn

Short lessons built around the exam content, with worked examples.

🧪

2. Practice

Quizzes and timed mock exams that show your strengths and gaps.

🤝

3. Belong

Ask, answer and share study tips with other candidates.

Yes. The platform is fully responsive and works in light or dark mode.
